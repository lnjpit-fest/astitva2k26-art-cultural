What is Astitva?

Five days the campus never forgets

Every September our college stops being a college and becomes a stage — concerts at sunset, debates that run hot, matches that go down to the last ball, and a josh that never goes down. 1500+ students, 10+ events, one long weekend.

Entry is free for every student with a valid college ID. Come to compete, to perform, or just to be part of it.
