About me

About Author & Designer

Presenting a registration platform for the technical fest of LNJPIT Chhapra — 04 to 08 September 2026.

An Intro

Hey there, I am Aditya

This static-paged website is designed and developed by me, Aditya, Mathematics & Computing student. This platform is created to facilitate the registration process for the Astitva '26 fest, ensuring a seamless experience for all participants. This site is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and is hosted on GitHub Pages. It is designed to be responsive and user-friendly, providing easy access to event information, schedules, and registration forms. The goal of this platform is to enhance the overall experience of the fest attendees and make the registration process as smooth as possible. I just took design ideas from across the internet and implemented them in this project by adjusting them as per need. I hope you like it.
